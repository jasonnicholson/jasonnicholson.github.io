- `$FIX/repro/extra.py`: monotonicity of the Case 1 variant, Muller's order at a simple root, nearest-root choice in Cases 2 and 3.
- `$FIX/postfix/compare.jl`: runs the HEAD and fixed scripts' iterate computation and compares the histories bit for bit. Usage: `julia compare.jl ../repro/head_aa.jl new_aa.jl`.

## Choice made for F1

Option (a) from the review: keep the real-part variant for Case 1 but make it explicit in the script, the animation and the page.

- It is the smallest change. The iterates stay bit-identical, and the page describes what the animation does (D7).
- Option (b) would mean new starting values. For f = (x−1)²(x+2) the interpolating parabola has real roots only when the triple straddles the root suitably, so this would replace Mathews' example with a different one.
- Option (c) cannot be drawn faithfully: with complex iterates the parabola through them is complex too.
- The archive supports (a). Mathews' own Example 1 uses the same starting values (2.2, 2.1, 2.0) and also uses a real-arithmetic fallback when the discriminant is negative (see Legacy archive). The difference is that his fallback sets the square root to 0, while this script takes the real part.
